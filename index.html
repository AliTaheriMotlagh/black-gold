The real map, at real scale

Fight across 4,000 km of the real Middle East — from the Nile to the Caspian, the Bosphorus to the Gulf of Aden. Real elevation, Sentinel-2 satellite imagery, 41 nations, 150 cities with real populations, roads, rivers and 37 real oil and gas fields.

  • Zoom from a single tank to the whole theater; units become NATO-style symbols on the strategic map.
  • Distances are real: armies road-march in convoys and deploy at the front.
  • Capture cities and the border moves with you.

Modern warfare, modelled

24 units and 19 structures built around today's battlefield: FPV kamikaze drones, Shahed-style loitering munitions, armed MALE drones, MLRS, long-range SAMs that intercept ballistic missiles, C-RAM, electronic warfare, laser air defence, stealth bombers, frigates and submarines.

  • Real projectile physics: lead-predicted shells, ballistic arcs, guided missiles with proximity fuses.
  • Every rocket, cruise and ballistic missile can be intercepted — or saturate your defences.
  • Artillery needs spotters; jets fly sorties and return to rearm.

Diplomacy that matters

Each player is a real country. Start from real 2025 alignments — the GCC, the Abraham Accords, Iran–Israel hostility — or from a clean slate. Your forces only fire on nations you are at war with.

  • Declare war with or without a casus belli — world opinion is watching.
  • Alliances, non-aggression pacts, trade deals, military access and sanctions.
  • Stability, war weariness and UN sanctions shape your economy.

FAQ

Is Black Gold free?

Yes. It runs free in any modern browser with no download or account.

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Yes. Tap to select and command, drag to pan, pinch to zoom, twist to rotate and long-press to box-select. Landscape is recommended on phones. You can also install it as an app from your browser.

Is the map real?

Yes. Terrain comes from real elevation data and satellite imagery; borders, cities, roads, rivers and oil fields come from open geographic datasets (Natural Earth, NASA, Copernicus Sentinel-2, AWS Terrain Tiles).

How does multiplayer work?

One player hosts and shares a code or link. Players connect directly (WebRTC). Every peer runs the same deterministic simulation in lockstep, so all commanders see exactly the same war.
